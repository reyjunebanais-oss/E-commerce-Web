Understanding Passive Electronic Components

The foundational elements of electrical circuit design

What are Passive Components?

Passive electronic components are elements that do not require an external power source to operate. Unlike active components (such as transistors or microcontrollers), passive devices cannot amplify signal power or generate energy. Instead, they control, store, dissipate, or release electrical energy within a circuit.

Key Types of Passive Components

Resistors

Limit the flow of electric current and drop voltage levels within a circuit by converting excess electrical energy into heat.

Capacitors

Store electrical energy temporarily in an electric field. They block direct current (DC) while allowing alternating current (AC) to pass through.

Inductors

Store energy in a magnetic field when electric current flows through them, resisting changes in current flow.

Transformers

Transfer electrical energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction, stepping voltage levels up or down.
