Understanding Sensors

Devices that detect and measure changes in the environment

What are Sensors?

Sensors are electronic devices that detect changes in physical conditions such as temperature, light, motion, pressure, sound, and humidity. They convert these changes into electrical signals that can be measured and processed by electronic systems.

Key Types of Sensors

Temperature Sensors

Measure temperature changes in the surrounding environment. They are commonly used in air conditioners, refrigerators, computers, and industrial systems.

Light Sensors

Detect the amount of light in an environment. They are used in automatic lights, cameras, smartphones, and display brightness control.

Motion Sensors

Detect movement of people or objects. They are commonly used in security systems, automatic doors, alarms, and smart devices.

Humidity Sensors

Measure the amount of moisture in the air. They are used in weather stations, smart homes, agriculture, and climate-control systems.

Pressure Sensors

Detect and measure pressure in gases or liquids. They are used in vehicles, industrial equipment, medical devices, and weather systems.

Ultrasonic Sensors

Use sound waves above the range of human hearing to detect distance and objects. They are commonly used in robots, parking systems, and obstacle detection.
